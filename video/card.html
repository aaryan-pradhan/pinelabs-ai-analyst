Pine Labs · Case Study 02

Enterprise AI Business Analyst

==============================================================================
Customer context

A payments business has transaction, merchant, bank, payment-method and settlement data spread across several datasets. Leaders ask questions like "why did success rates change?", "which segments drove the decline?", "what should we do?".

==============================================================================
Goal

Build an AI analyst that turns a natural-language business question into safe, reproducible data analysis and an evidence-backed answer.
