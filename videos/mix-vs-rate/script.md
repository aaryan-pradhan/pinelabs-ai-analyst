## Why success rate fell in week 6

<!-- beat: mosaic names="UPI,cards,wallets,net banking" before="61.3,28.5,4.1,6.1" after="43.9,19.8,15.9,20.4" rates="95.5,91.5,79.9,78.2" avg="92.7,88.7" mix="-3.99" rate="-0.03" -->

Nothing broke.

Each bar is a payment method.

Its width is its share of traffic; its height is its success rate.

From week 5 to week 6, the heights don't move.

Only the widths do.

Traffic moved from $\text{UPI}$ and $\text{cards}$ to $\text{net banking}$ and $\text{wallets}$, which always succeed less.

That alone pulls the average down.

The mix effect is −4 points.

The rate effect is almost zero.
